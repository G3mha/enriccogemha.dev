Best in Class: Sensor Test, shared with S.A.R.T., at RoboCup 2022 in <span class="nowrap">Bangkok <Flag country="th" label="Thailand" /></span>, and Best in Class: Exploration, the autonomous mapping test, at RoboCup 2023 in <span class="nowrap">Bordeaux <Flag country="fr" label="France" /></span>.

The 2023 entry ran 2D LiDAR mapping, and [Insper's write-up](https://web.archive.org/web/20230716162416/https://www.insper.edu.br/noticias/insper-dynamics-conquista-4o-lugar-no-mundial-de-robotica-na-franca/) says Insper Dynamics was the only team to map the arena on its own. A search-and-rescue robot is scored on finding victims in a collapsed structure it has never entered, so the robot has to build its own map as it goes and remember where it has been.

The 2022 award is how I became president of Insper's robotics and AI club the following year. As president, I got the club a $40,000 budget by rebuilding the campus access app in exchange for it, rather than asking for the money.
