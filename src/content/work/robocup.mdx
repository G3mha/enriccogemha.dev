Best in Class: Sensing at RoboCup 2022 in <span class="nowrap">Bangkok <Flag country="th" label="Thailand" /></span>, and Best in Class: Mapping and Autonomous Navigation at RoboCup 2023 in <span class="nowrap">Bordeaux <Flag country="fr" label="France" /></span>.

The 2023 entry ran 2D LiDAR mapping, which the category hadn't seen before. A search-and-rescue robot is scored on finding victims in a collapsed structure it has never entered, so the robot has to build its own map as it goes and remember where it has been. Camera-only entries could identify a victim but couldn't reliably say where it was.

The 2022 award is how I became president of Insper's robotics and AI club the following year. As president, I got the club a $40,000 budget by rebuilding the campus access app in exchange for it, rather than asking for the money.
